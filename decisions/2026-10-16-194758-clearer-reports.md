# Clearer review and handover reports

Issue: #32

Fresh-eyes reports and handovers were long and put everything at the same weight, so what Skye had to act on was mixed in with what was only there for the record. Both now lead with what needs her.

- **Fresh-eyes buckets are Fix, Needs you and footnotes,** replacing Act on, Consider, Noted and Dropped. Every Fix gets a suggested fix, and every Needs you a recommendation, so the report says what to do as well as what's wrong. Noted and Dropped stay, since they let Skye overrule the judgement, but fold into a `<details>` block.
- **The handover reuses the words:** Fixed (with how, why and the commit's sha), Needs you, and the footnotes, so the same word means the same thing in both. Needs you comes first because it's what Skye acts on. Out-of-plan Fix findings move to Needs you, recommending a new issue.
- **No shared template** between the two: the handover adds what fresh-eyes can't know (the fix's sha, whether a finding is in the plan).
- **Shorter handover:** Review it is one `git diff` line, and What changed is one line per file, with files that share a change sharing a line. The diff holds the detail. #20's handover, rewritten this way, went from 694 words to 330.
- **Plain paths in chat as well as in the comment,** so chat shows exactly what's posted. Links in chat were clickable, but made the two differ, and links on GitHub would be broken while the branch is local.
- **`/ship` still finds what it reads:** the `## Handover` heading, the full sha on Covers, What changed's file notes and the manual steps are unchanged.

Left out: rewording older decisions that mention Act on, Consider and Noted.
